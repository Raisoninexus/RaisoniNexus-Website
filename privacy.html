Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how Raisoni Nexus handles information when you browse the website, create a student account, save or download a resource, or submit a material request. Raisoni Nexus is a student-led academic resource platform and is not an official college website unless formally authorized.

Information used by the site

  • Account information: If you register or sign in, the authentication service processes your email address and account credentials. A profile may also include your name and optional branch or semester information. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider, not displayed to site administrators.
  • Student activity: When available, bookmarks and resource-download records are associated with your account to provide saved-resource features and platform reporting.
  • Material requests: If you request study material, the form collects the name and email address you provide, the material requested, and any details you include.
  • Technical information: The website host and service providers may process connection and diagnostic information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the site. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.

How information is used

Information is used to operate student accounts, provide requested features, organize resource access, respond to material requests, protect the service, and maintain or improve the website. Access should be limited to people who need the information to administer the service.

Service providers and international processing

The website is hosted on Vercel. Supabase provides application database, authentication, and storage services. If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose to use it, Google processes the sign-in according to Google's privacy terms. These providers may process information in locations outside your own. Review their current privacy terms for details.

Advertising and cookies

The site currently includes AdSense verification code, but ad serving is not active while Google reviews the site. If AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Where permitted, Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to show ads based on visits to this site or other sites. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about other opt-out choices at aboutads.info. This policy must be updated to reflect the actual advertising services and consent requirements before ads are enabled.

Storage, security, and retention

Some site preferences or authentication state may be stored in your browser. The service and its providers use technical measures intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information should be retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above, operational requirements, or applicable obligations. Contact the site to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information; some account data may need to be handled through the account provider.

Children and student accounts

The service is intended for students and general academic use. Do not submit another person's personal information unless you are authorized to do so. If you believe information about you was submitted inappropriately, contact the site so it can be reviewed.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when site features or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email RaisoniNexus@gmail.com.

Important: This page is an operational privacy notice, not legal advice. The site owner should confirm that it accurately reflects the live Supabase, hosting, email, analytics, advertising, and consent configuration before applying for AdSense or collecting additional information.
